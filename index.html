GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about JDR Farm

What does JDR Farm make?

We make coconut oil, groundnut oil, palm sprout powder, moringa leaf powder and traditional ingredients at our farm near Palani, Tamil Nadu.

Are your products certified organic?

We use organic growing practices, but our products are not certified organic. Contact us if you would like to know more about how a particular product is grown or prepared.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. We deliver all our products across India. Send us a WhatsApp enquiry for the current price, availability and delivery details.

Can I visit JDR Farm to buy products?

Yes. You can visit us in Ponnapuram, Ayakudi (PO), Palani, Dindigul District, Tamil Nadu 624613 and buy directly from the farm.

How do I place an order?

Choose a product and pack size, then use its WhatsApp enquiry link. We will confirm availability, price and delivery details directly with you before an order is placed.
