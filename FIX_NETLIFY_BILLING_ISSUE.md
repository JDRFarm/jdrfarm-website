## ✅ Solution: Pay Overdue Invoice

### Step 1: Pay the Overdue Invoice

1. **In Netlify Dashboard:**
   - Go to: **Usage & billing** (you're already there)
   - Scroll to: **"Overdue invoices"** section
   - You should see: **$8.42** due on Jan 4

2. **Click:** **"Contact billing support"** or look for **"Pay now"** button

3. **Or go directly to:**
   - https://app.netlify.com/teams/JDRFarm/billing/invoices
   - Find the overdue invoice ($8.42)
   - Click to pay it

### Step 2: Update Payment Method

1. **In Netlify Dashboard:**
   - Go to: **Usage & billing** → **Billing details**
   - Section: **"Payment information"**
   - You see: **Visa ending in 3461**

2. **Click:** **"Change payment method"** or **"Update payment method"**

3. **Options:**
   - **If card is still valid:** Verify it's active and has funds
   - **If card expired:** Add new payment method
   - **Update card details** if needed

4. **Save** the payment method

### Step 3: Wait for Reactivation

**Important:** After updating payment:
- Netlify will charge your card
- **Can take up to 24 hours** to reactivate
- Your projects will come back online automatically

**What Netlify says:**
> "Updating your payment information will not immediately charge your card. It could take up to 24 hours for Netlify to charge your card and restore your account"
